Field notes

Making things slowly.

JUL 2026

Why start with a puzzle?

Because an honest game needs no explanation: you either want one more turn, or you do not. Pattern Atlas explores fitting pieces; Lantern Grove explores quiet deduction. Signal Stack asks whether one good rule can keep generating the next decision. Pebble Choir turns collision into a tiny instrument.

JUL 31 2026

Leaderboards, quietly.

Two games learned to remember a score today. Signal Stack and Pebble Choir now keep a small board — not for crowning winners, just for noticing that you came back and tried once more. A score only gets sent when it beats your own best, which felt like the right amount of pressure. Finally.
